Explore CMS measure OP-10, Abdomen CT Use of Contrast Material.

What OP-10 measures

OP-10 — Abdomen CT Use of Contrast Material reports the percentage of a hospital’s outpatient abdominal CT examinations performed both with and without contrast. Looking at both sets of images can be clinically appropriate in some situations, so a higher percentage is not, by itself, proof of poor care.

This CMS measure concerns one specific CT practice; it is not a measure of every CT scan or of overall hospital quality. The charts below show hospitals with an available OP-10 score in the July 1, 2024–June 30, 2025 reporting period.
