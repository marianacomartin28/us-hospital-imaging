Explore Hospital Imaging Data

Compare CMS outpatient imaging measure distributions and state or territory summaries.

Explore the reported measures

These interactive charts use the CMS reporting period July 1, 2024–June 30, 2025. Each record is a hospital’s reported percentage for one measure. Select or hide measures in the legend, and hover over marks for details.

Hospital score distributions

Each bar counts hospitals whose reported percentage falls in that score range. The measures are overlaid for comparison; use the menu to view either measure alone.

Compare state and territory medians

Bars show the median reported percentage among hospitals with an available score in each state or territory. A median summarizes a group; it is not a hospital rating, and differences can reflect many factors beyond care quality.

Interactive State Explorer

Choose a state or territory and an imaging measure to explore individual hospital scores. The chart updates instantly without reloading the page.

The chart displays available hospital-reported percentages, not a ranking of clinical quality. Hospitals with missing scores are excluded. Differences may reflect patient populations, reporting practices, and clinical circumstances.

Measure guide

  • OP-10 — Abdomen CT Use of Contrast Material: The reported percentage of outpatient abdominal CT examinations performed both with and without contrast.
  • OP-39 — Breast Cancer Screening Recall Rates: The reported percentage of screening mammograms followed by diagnostic imaging within 45 days.

The measures describe different processes and should not be read as a single score of hospital quality. The CMS dataset provides hospital-level reported percentages; it does not explain why a particular hospital’s value differs.
